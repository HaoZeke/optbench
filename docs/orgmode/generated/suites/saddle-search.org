   <h2>Pt heptamer island</h2>
   <dl class="bench-rule">
   <dt>Stop</dt><dd>norm of the force at or below 0.001 eV/Angstrom</dd>
   <dt>Goal</dt><dd>27 connected saddles below 1.5 eV</dd>
   <dt>v2</dt><dd>par2-wilson-bootstrap</dd>
   <dt>v2 cost</dt><dd>force_calls divided by total_searches in that repetition</dd>
   </dl>
   <p class="bench-smoke">A 2-search SoftSaddle smoke is not in this rank. The April 2019 adaptive_HSphere-lbfgs-davidson-sm50 entry records 423491.1 force calls.</p>
