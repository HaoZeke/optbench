   <h2>Pt heptamer island</h2>
   <dl class="bench-rule">
   <dt>Stop</dt><dd>L2 norm of the force at or below 0.001 eV/Angstrom</dd>
   <dt>Goal</dt><dd>27 connected saddles below 1.5 eV</dd>
   <dt>v2</dt><dd>par2-wilson-bootstrap</dd>
   <dt>Search budget</dt><dd>5064</dd>
   <dt>v2 cost</dt><dd>campaign total of harness force calls through the search that records the 27th good saddle below 1.5 eV</dd>
   <dt>Score note</dt><dd>search_budget is the budget of one displacement search: climb 1000, 64 calls of overshoot, and two endpoint minimizations of 2000. The initial minimization is outside that count. search_budget is not the budget of the 27-saddle campaign. A search at or above 5064 fails that search and the campaign continues. The ranked cost is the harness force-call total from the start through the search that records the 27th good saddle below 1.5 eV. A campaign that never records that saddle is scored by the harness force calls it spent. There is no campaign cap, so that spent total is not doubled. A result that stores one row per displacement search, and does not store that campaign total, stays off this ranked table.</dd>
   </dl>
   <p class="bench-note">A 2-search SoftSaddle check is outside this rank. The April 2019 adaptive_HSphere-lbfgs-davidson-sm50 entry records 423491.1 force calls.</p>
   <p>Ranked by the v2 PAR-2 mean of the harness total. Only the newest full version of a template is in this table. A short run, including a one-start asv run, is not.</p>
   <p class="bench-gaps">Short runs stay off the ranked table.</p>
