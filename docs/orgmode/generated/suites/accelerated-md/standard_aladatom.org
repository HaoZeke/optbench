   <dt>Products</dt><dd>5000</dd>

   <dt>v2</dt><dd>par2-bootstrap</dd>
   <dt>Note</dt><dd>Aluminum adatom on Al(100) at 150 K. The reactant and the Voter-Chen EAM are the same as the 250 K problem. The reference escape rate is the parallel-replica row with Langevin friction 0.001 per fs. One repetition is one AKMC catalogue. The stated sample is three seeds. The product probabilities are the 250 K molecular dynamics standard, which is the only published catalogue.</dd>
