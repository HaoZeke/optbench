   <dt>Starts</dt><dd>100</dd>
   <dt>Potential</dt><dd>binary-lennard-jones</dd>
   <dt>v2</dt><dd>par2-bootstrap</dd>
   <dt>Note</dt><dd>The archived files record energies. They do not record force calls. Success is an energy at or below the improved Cambridge minimum plus 0.001. The cap is the archived evaluation budget of 2000000.</dd>
   </dl>
