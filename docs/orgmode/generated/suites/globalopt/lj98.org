   <h2>lj98</h2>
   <dl class="bench-rule">
   <dt>Cap</dt><dd>1000000000 force calls</dd>
   <dt>Stop</dt><dd>within 0.001 of the known minimum</dd>
   <dt>Starts</dt><dd>100</dd>
   <dt>Potential</dt><dd>lennard-jones</dd>
   <dt>v2</dt><dd>par2-bootstrap</dd>
   <dt>Note</dt><dd>Success is an energy at or below the Cambridge Cluster Database N=98 minimum plus 0.001. The start set is the 100 numbered coordinate files. The cap is this file&#x27;s convergence.max_force_calls, 1000000000. That cap is above the archived nosym energy-call mean 5.98857238e+07 plus ten times its standard deviation 6.21561817e+07.</dd>
   <dt>Contract</dt><dd>The columns are the keys shared by every archived file.</dd>
   </dl>
   <p>Rows are ranked by cost inside each method family and on one overall table. A run short of the full set, or a run that is not the stock method, stays off the table.</p>
