OptBench ranks geometry optimizers, saddle searches, and global
optimization by the cost of a finished answer.
Chill, Stevenson, Ruehle, Shang, Xiao, Farrell, Wales, and Henkelman
set the problems in J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2014, 10, 5476-5482
([[https://doi.org/10.1021/ct5008718][doi:10.1021/ct5008718]]).
The table on this page is one thousand Lennard-Jones 38 minimizations.
A run in the archive counts as finished when it stops under 10000 force
calls.
A new run also has to bring the norm of the force to 0.01.

in the contents.
