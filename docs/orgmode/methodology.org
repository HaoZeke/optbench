#+TITLE: Methodology
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil author:nil

PAR-2, the success rate, and archived scores are defined here.
The other pages link to this one.

* PAR-2

PAR-2 is the mean cost of the runs.
A run that finishes counts as the calls it used.
A run that fails counts as twice the cap.

* Success rate

The success rate is the share of runs that finished.
A dash means the row did not record a success rate.

* Archived scores

An archived score is the average of the runs that finished under the cap.
A run that hit the cap is a failure and is left out of that average.
An archived row is ranked by the average it published.
It has no PAR-2 score, so the leaderboard shows a dash.

* Best, median, and the bar

Best is the lowest cost for that code on that problem.
Median is the median of that code's costs on that problem.
Each problem has one log scale, shared by every code on that problem.
The scale is labeled "cost (force calls, log scale)".
It runs from the decade at or below the lowest positive cost to the one at or above the highest.
A shorter bar is a lower cost.
The lowest positive cost is drawn in copper.
A bar has no minimum length.
A cost at or below zero is left off the scale, and the sentence under the chart says so.

* Performance profile

The performance profile is a Dolan-Moré curve (Dolan, E. D. and Moré, J. J., Math. Program. 91:201-213, 2002).
The cost ratio runs left to right.
The height of a curve is the fraction of starts solved within that ratio of the best start.
A higher curve is better.
When more than six codes are drawn, the colored lines are the six largest areas under the curve.
The other rows are thin lines.
A family profile uses the same definition on the rows in one method family, and the best start is the best cost inside that family.
It is drawn when the family has at least two curves with a shared start set.
Tau is 1, 2, 4, 8, and 16.

* Version history

A history plot is the recorded PAR-2 and the mean force calls against the code version.
Versions run left to right in numeric order.
A row with no PAR-2 is left off that panel.
No line is drawn across a missing value.

* Starts

Each mark on the strip plot is one recorded start.
The box is the quartiles of those force calls.
A blue point finished and a vermillion cross is a recorded failure.
The vertical offset only separates marks.
The force-call count is the recorded one.

* Success rate bars

Each bar is the success rate of one ranked row, in rank order.
A row that did not record a success rate is left off the chart.

* Cost distribution

The cost distribution counts ranked rows in bins of their rank cost.
Bins are logarithmic when every cost is positive and the largest is at least ten times the smallest.
Otherwise the bins are linear.
A row with no cost on that scale is left off the chart, and the caption says how many.

* Other columns

Hessians is the mean analytic Hessian count.
FD calls is that count times one force call per coordinate.
The per-image mean divides a band's total by the number of moving images.
The ranked table leaves out a run that used fewer starts than the full set or a method other than the standard one.

* Missing numbers

A dash means the problem file or the row has no value for that number.
The footnote on the page says which.
